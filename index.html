01 / About

Curiosity grounded in engineering.

A student portfolio documenting projects, technical interests, and ongoing learning.

My interests sit where software systems meet the physical world. I enjoy exploring how components interact, how systems respond to failure, and how engineering decisions affect reliability, efficiency, and real-world behavior.

Through independent projects, I have been learning by designing, implementing, testing, and documenting ideas. I treat each project as an opportunity to ask better questions, understand trade-offs, and improve my engineering judgment.

This website is a living record of that process—not a list of claims, but a place to share work and ideas as they develop.
